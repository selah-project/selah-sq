# NOTES — The Selah Albanian Rendering (shqip)

Chair 101 of THE 127. Lit 2026-09-27, mid-morning local.

This file is the working record of the burn: every pin, every fault, every
acquittal, in the order it was found. It is written **as the fire burns**, not
afterwards — a note held back until the end is a note lost.

Read `PROVENANCE.md` for what this rendering is and is not. Read
`CONTRIBUTING.md` before correcting anything, because **some of what looks wrong
here is deliberate.**

---

## §1 — Why Albanian, and what cleared it

Rank 101 of THE 127, chosen in strict order. It cleared **both** floors at the
previous chair's levels — 19 of 20 on the plain-noun probe, 69% on the stability
probe. It is **the first chair the stability floor picked rather than explained**:
on chairs 99 and 100 that probe was a diagnosis written after the fact.

## §2 — Three facts that each invert a rule of chair 100

- **`ë` and `ç` are LETTERS of this alphabet.** Chair 100 wrote no diacritics at
  all, and a check there stripped every accented character as stray decoration.
  Copied here it would have convicted correct Albanian on every other line.
  **It was deleted, not adapted** — and one experiment had already written the
  caution into its own docstring a chair earlier: *on a chair with diacritics this
  rule could not exist.* This is the chair it was written for.
- **`l` and `ll` are two different letters**, and the digraphs
  `dh gj ll nj rr sh th xh zh` are single letters. Chair 100 collapsed doubled
  consonants; here that would be destructive.
- **Albanian permits final consonants**, so `Elohim` stands where chair 100
  needed a vowel appended.

## §3 — The first verses read right

`Dhe Kenani jetoi shtatëdhjetë vjet, dhe lindi ⟨את⟩ Mahalalelin` — marker kept,
numbers from the rails table, `ë` correct, names in the definite accusative.
Genesis 5:27 gives **969 for Metushelah**, the verse where chair 100 wrote noise
for the name.

## §4 — The first-hour gate DELETES a check, which is why it could not be copied

The slot the accent check vacated went to a **Gheg register reading list.** Gheg
is Albanian and its speakers are millions; the written standard is Tosk. So a Gheg
form is **the wrong register, not a foreign word**, and it is surfaced, never
counted as an error. A dialect of the target language cannot be a bleed.

## §5 — Two bleed candidates struck by the first run, and I had written the rule above them

The bleed list holds only grammatical words, because Albanian carries real loans
from Italian, Turkish and Greek and **those loans are Albanian.** I wrote that
rule at the top of the list and then left two entries that break it:

- **`the`** — listed as English. **Albanian `the` is the 2nd person singular past
  of `thashë`, *you said*.** `ותאמר` → `dhe iu the` at Genesis 44:21 and 44:23,
  where the brothers quote Joseph. A perfect rendering, and the entry would have
  convicted every quoted *you said* in the Tanakh.
- **`ma`** — listed as Italian. **Albanian `ma` is the fused clitic `më`+`e`, *it
  to me*.** `תנה` → `ma jep` at Genesis 30:26, Jacob to Laban.

**Writing the rule is not applying it.**

## §6 — The `Jehova` cluster, and a forbid must name the ROOT

Four of seven early Name faults were `Jehovai` ×4 and `për Jehovain`, all in
**Exodus 13:14–16** — three consecutive verses. The table named `Jehova`, the
nominative; the chair produced the case forms. **Albanian suffixes case onto the
stem, so a forbid must name the root** — where chair 100's prefix-marking meant a
named word sufficed.

**The pin did not hold.** `Jehova` forms kept appearing for two hours after it:
1 Kings 16:33 and 16:34, Jeremiah 28:9, Joel 4:11. Recorded rather than smoothed.

## §7 — The numeral order: the row was right and the flow recomposed it

Hebrew writes the **unit before the ten** — `חמש וששים`, *five and sixty* = 65.
Albanian writes the **ten before the unit**. The machine kept Hebrew's order, and
because Albanian builds tens by suffixing, the result is **a well-formed Albanian
number with the wrong value**: 65→56, 95→59, 365→356, 32→42, 403→433.

**In every case the gloss row had both words right.** `חמש → pesë` and
`וששים → dhe gjashtëdhjetë`, correct; only the flow moved the `-dhjetë`. So the
fault lives in one surface and not the other, and **a check reading only the row
would call the chair clean.**

## §8 — The lamb: it knew the word and scattered it anyway

`שה` had **29 appearances and 23 different renderings**, and the three worst are
in **Exodus 12, where the Passover is instituted** — `një` (*a*), `mijën` (*the
thousand*), and `krye` (*head*) at Genesis 30:32.

**And the chair knew the word.** At Genesis 22:7–8 — *where is the lamb* /
*Elohim will provide himself a lamb* — it wrote `qengji` and `qengjin`, correct.
The scatter was the fault, not ignorance; the same consistency failure that cost
chair 99 a day.

The rule was written naming Isaiah 53:7 as not yet landed. **That verse came down
nineteen minutes later and reads `si qengj ⟨i çuar⟩ në therje, dhe si dele
përpara rrojtareve të saj heshti`.** One instance, not a proof.

## §9 — `שאול` is three referents and was written as two

The king was **`Saul` 255 times and `Shaul` 42**, in the same two books. Before
pinning it I checked the split by book rather than assuming: **`Shaul` in Genesis
46:10 is Simeon's son and is correct.** Isaiah's one `Shaulit` is *Gibeah of
Saul* at 10:29, the 42nd instance of the same fault, not a Sheol error — and
`Sheol` itself is right 8 times out of 8.

## §10 — The Name is declined 910 times, on two paradigms, and that is filed not fixed

The flow carries the Name 2,510 times and **910 are inflected** — `Jahvesë` 521,
`Jahves` 225, `Jahven` 83, `Jahvenë` 67, `Jahveut` 10, `Jahveja` 3, `Jahvei` 1.
**899 on a feminine paradigm, 11 on a masculine one.**

**This is not a fault I can call.** `fjala e Jahvesë` is how Albanian says *the
word of Jahve*, and forcing the bare nominative there reads as broken Albanian,
not as reverence. What is reportable is that **two systems are running at once.**

And the same question recurs on other transliterations: `hesed` · `hesedi` ·
`hesed-i`, and `Adon-it` · `Eloah-ut` with a hyphen against `Jahvesë` without one,
and `Sheolin` at Isaiah 28:15 against `Sheol-it` at 28:18.

One invariant fell out free: **the Name appears in the flow exactly as often as in
the row, 2,510 both sides.** It is neither added nor dropped.

## §11 — The erasure check was blind to every declined form

The check whose whole job is catching a substitute in the Name's place ended in
`(?![\p{L}])` — it refuses to match when a letter follows. So `Jehovai`,
`Jehovain`, `Jehovait` walked through, and it printed **erasure 0** for two hours
while four post-pin faults sat on disk. They were found by listing file times.

**A word boundary in a case language is not where the stem ends.** Three checks
had the same blind spot within one hour.

## §12 — And widening it convicted a correct Albanian verb

The widened boundary immediately flagged **`Godit`** at Amos 9:1 and 2 Kings
6:18. **`godit` is the ordinary Albanian verb *to strike*.** `הך הכפתור` is
*Smite the lintel*; `הך נא את הגוי הזה בסנורים` is *Smite this people with
blindness* — **and that verse ends `i goditi me verbëri` in the same breath.**
`God` plus the tail `-it` collides with it, and capitalisation cannot separate
them because the imperative heads a sentence.

*A word lawful in the language cannot be convicted by a pattern that does not
know the language* — **and this time I reached it by loosening a boundary, which
is a new way into the same error.**

## §13 — The Hebrew surface is data, not output — and sometimes it is output

`את lost 1` fired for the first time after 6,297 clean seats. The cause was
**`אתha`** at Isaiah 41:9 — `אתה`, *thou*, with the letter `ה` written as its own
**Latin name**. Stripping non-Hebrew left `את`, so a marker was demanded of a word
that never carried one, and the gloss `ti je` was right.

**65 surfaces hold Latin letters**, always the same shape: a Hebrew letter
replaced by its transliteration mid-word (`תעבod`, `לאrץ`, `יכשלu`), or the whole
word Latinized (`koll` for `כל`, **`zekhub` for `זהב`, gold**). Exactly one per
verse, never clustered.

**This is not confined to this chair: 574 across 42 of 103 chairs**, and elsewhere
the field holds the machine's own scaffolding — `placeholder`, `_unused`,
`_error`, and in one chair **`יהוה_error`**, the Name with `_error` appended.

## §14 — The marker fabricated in the flow

Jeremiah 51:52's Hebrew is `ופקדתי על פסיליה` — *I will visit **upon** her graven
images* — with **no `את` in the verse.** `על` is glossed empty, no token carries
the marker, **and the flow says `⟨את⟩`.** 2 Samuel 20:9 is the same: no `את`, one
marker. 179 verses.

The check for it was wrong twice first — 680, then 1,236, then 179 — and what
fixed it was reading the verses, not adjusting the threshold. The **dropped**
direction turned out to be the ⟨את⟩-family question and not a fault at all.

## §15 — The fixed-terms audit, and the chair was right where the rails were rigid

Every fixed term against the Hebrew, 1,590 tokens. **The graded band came out
clean except the lamb.** Everything else was the rails being too flat for the
Hebrew's own range:

`ברית` → `Berit` is **Baal-Berith**, a proper name · `משיח` → `i vajosuri` is the
**participle**, over a priest, over Saul, and once over a **shield** · `נביא` at
1 Samuel 9:7 is the **hiphil of `בוא`**, *what shall we bring* · `רוח` twice is
**`רֶוַח`**, space and relief · `מנחה` is **`מְנֻחָה`** *rest*, and **Manahath**,
and Jacob's **present** to Joseph · `נפש` is *life* and *creature*, with **zero
faults in 252 tokens.**

Chair 99 taught that *the rails can be right and the chair still wrong.* **This is
the inverse, and it is harder to see because the rails look like the authority.**

## §16 — A fabrication detector needs no word list, because you cannot list what was invented

`ofrece` is **Spanish**, and Spanish is not a neighbour of Albanian, so no bleed
list could hold it. The inverse works: **a real word of the language recurs; an
invented one appears once.** 20,103 distinct words, 1,679 hapax, and one condition
makes it usable — report a hapax only when **no word occurring more than twice
shares its first four letters.**

What it found: 3 Cyrillic homoglyphs · 5 **non-Albanian letters** (`ö ä ü î ş`) ·
one entire welded clause · **10 words of grammatical metalanguage** —
`infinitivit`, `foljore`, `rasës`, `absolut`, `intensiv` — **the chair talking
about its own work inside the verse.** And `⟨token⟩` at Psalm 96:9 and 96:10.

And its widest band over-reached: **`durim`, `hudhra`, `fiqe`, `sqepi` are good
all-ASCII Albanian.** *All-ASCII means English* is a false conviction.

## §17 — Whether a pin took, and three claims withdrawn

Four pins in ninety minutes, checked four different ways, and **three of my own
claims failed when one instrument checked them all.** I called the numeral pin
TAKEN on a window of 1,703 verses with 0 faults; the next 2,450 produced nine.

**The denominator was the error, and it is a law: a fault whose base rate varies
by BOOK cannot be banded by verse count.** Number faults live in genealogies and
censuses; `שאול` lives in 1–2 Samuel; `שה` in Exodus 12. The window in question
was burning **Isaiah and Jeremiah** — number-sparse and Saul-free. Counting
**opportunities** instead of verses moved Saul's expectation by **30×**, from 9.3
to 0.3.

Standing: numeral order **undetermined** · lamb **not enough evidence** · Saul
**not enough** · `Jehova` **not taken.** None demonstrated.

## §18 — What the deterministic repair refuses to touch

64 verses are repaired mechanically: `Shaul`→`Saul`, empty `⟨⟩` replaced by a
space, three angle brackets closed. **Everything else is a re-press**, because
guessing what the machine meant is authoring, not repairing.

And its dry run caught two bugs in itself. Deleting an empty bracket with its
leading space welded `mbushur ⟨⟩tokën` into **`mbushurtokën`** — 23 verses.
And closing `⟨token>` would have made the English word **syntactically valid and
therefore invisible.** **The malformed bracket was the signal.**

## §19 — The census, and what is held

2,495 convicted · **444 pressable** · 2,051 held-only. The convicted count is
never the call count.

every token check reads as clean — is **6 here, where chair 99 shipped 52 and
chair 100 had 58.**

Held: the Name's declension (1,798, filed) · absorbed glosses (189, **lawful —

corrupted surfaces (65, ingest) · the ⟨את⟩ ruling (46, four chairs deep) · and
`Shaul` (45, which the deterministic pass settles).
